Android App Testing FAQ

Are the testers real people?
+

Yes. 100%. We never use emulators, bots, or automated scripts. Every tester uses a physical Android device and has an established, aged Google account to ensure you pass Google's strict Play Integrity checks.

Do I need to change my app's code?
+

No. You simply add our tester email group to your Google Play Console "Closed Testing" track and share the opt-in URL with us. We handle the installation and testing natively through the Play Store app.

What if my app gets rejected?
+

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee. If your production access is rejected specifically for "insufficient testing activity" while using our service correctly, we will either provide a full refund or run a completely new 14-day test at zero cost.

Can I provide specific testing instructions?
+

Yes. If your app requires a specific login, registration flow, or has a core feature you want tested heavily, you can provide testing instructions during the checkout process.
