FAQ

Still have questions?
Are the 12 testers real people or bots?
All 12 testers are 100% real humans operating physical Android devices. We never use bots, scripts or emulators. Google's detection is sophisticated — our 99.8% success rate exists because we don't compromise on this. You can verify device models in your Play Console dashboard.
What if Google rejects my app after testing?
If the rejection is caused by the testing phase not meeting requirements, we repeat the entire 14-day cycle at no additional cost. If it fails again, you receive a 100% full refund. Note: refunds don't cover rejections caused by your app violating Google's content policies.
How does payment work exactly?
After you submit the order form, our team reviews your request and sends a secure payment link to your email within 30 minutes. You are never auto-charged. Testing only begins after payment is confirmed — this gives you a chance to ask questions before committing.
How fast does testing start?
Most orders have testers assigned within 12–24 hours of payment confirmation. In many cases testers appear in your Play Console the same day. You'll receive an email confirmation once testing has started.
Do you need my APK or developer account?
No. You only provide your app's package name or internal testing track link. You add our tester email addresses to your Play Console — we never request your account login, credentials or any sensitive access.
Do testers actually use the app every day?
Yes. Google tracks active session data, not just installs. Our testers are briefed to open and meaningfully use your app every day for all 14 days. You'll see daily session data confirming this in your Play Console dashboard.
Can I test multiple apps at once?
Yes — use the slider on the pricing section to select 3 or more apps. Testing can run in parallel or on a staggered schedule. The more apps you order, the lower the per-app rate drops (down to $15/app at 10 apps).
Can I choose specific devices?
Our testers use a wide variety of real Android phones across manufacturers and OS versions. For specific device requirements (e.g., Pixel-only, specific Android version), contact us via WhatsApp before ordering for a custom solution.
Is this against Google's terms of service?
No. Google's policy specifically requires real human testers — which is exactly what we provide. We fulfill the requirement legitimately, not circumvent it. Over 500 approved apps and zero account suspensions confirm our compliance record.
What happens after the 14 days?
We send you a step-by-step guide for submitting your production access request. Most approvals from Google arrive within 3–7 business days. Testers remain in your group unless removed, and you can then publish your app to Google Play's 3+ billion users.
