Welcome to 12 Testers for 14 Days. By using our website and services, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions.

1. Service Description

We provide a service that connects Android developers with physical device users to fulfill Google Play's 14-day closed testing requirement. We guarantee that 12 or more users will opt into your test and log activity over a consecutive 14-day period.

2. Developer Obligations

To successfully deliver our service, you agree to the following:

  • Your application must not violate any Google Play content policies (e.g., malware, illegal content).
  • You must set up the closed testing track properly within the Google Play Console prior to the testers opting in.
  • You will not remove or swap out testers mid-test, nor will you push updates that cause immediate app crashes hindering tester access.

3. Refunds and Guarantee

Our 100% approval guarantee covers our service execution. If Google rejects your production application specifically due to "insufficient testing activity," we will run a second 14-day test for free. If the second test fails for the same reason, you are entitled to a full refund. Please review our full Refund Policy for exact details and exclusions.

While we guarantee tester participation to fulfill Google's requirement, 12 Testers for 14 Days is not responsible for broader account bans, suspensions, or app rejections that arise from policy violations, code issues, or actions unrelated to testing volume.
