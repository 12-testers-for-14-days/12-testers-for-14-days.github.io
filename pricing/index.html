Everything About 12 Testers & App Publishing

When do I actually pay?

We've removed the risk. You submit your app details first. We manually assign our 12+ real human testers to your Play Console track. You log into your console, verify they have opted in, and only then do we send you a secure payment link.

Are your testers real humans?

Yes. 100%. We never use emulators, bots, or automated scripts. Every tester uses a physical Android device and has an established Google account to ensure you pass Google's strict Play Integrity checks.

What happens if Google rejects my app?

If your production access is rejected specifically for "insufficient testing activity" while using our service correctly, we will either provide a full refund or run a completely new 14-day test at zero cost.

Do I need to change my app's code?

No. You simply add our tester email group to your Google Play Console "Closed Testing" track and share the opt-in URL with us. We handle the rest.
