FAQ

Asked before every order.

If yours isn't here, ask us directly. A human answers, fast.

Is this against Google's rules?
No. Google's policy requires 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days on new personal developer accounts — it doesn't dictate how you find those testers. We provide real people on real, physical Android devices who genuinely install and use your app.
What is Google Play's 14-day closed testing requirement?
New personal developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days before the "Apply for production" step unlocks in Play Console.
What if my app is not approved?
If Google rejects production access due to insufficient testing activity, we refund you in full or re-run the 14-day test at no charge — whichever you prefer.
Are the testers real people?
Yes. Every tester in our network is a real person using a physical Android device with a mature Google account. We never use emulators, bots, or automated scripts.
When do I make the payment?
You only pay after we have manually assigned our 12 testers to your closed testing track and you have verified them in your Play Console dashboard.
Do I have to do anything during the 14 days?
No. Once testers opt in, our team manages daily activity end to end. You can check progress in Play Console any time, but there's nothing you need to do until day 15.
What happens after the 14 days are over?
You'll receive an engagement report, and Play Console's "Apply for production" button unlocks. From there you complete Google's short questionnaire and submit for review.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We process secure payments via Payoneer. We accept all major debit and credit cards worldwide. No subscription — you pay once per order.
